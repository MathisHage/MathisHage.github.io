## Overview
The aim of this project was to dive into a research paper about random graph theory. I selected the paper [Increasing the chromatic number of a random graph](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.1748) by N. Alon and B. Sudakov, as I was looking for a more theoretical question to study. The objectives were multiple:
- Summarize the paper to demonstrate understanding of the question, method of resolution, and results;
- Give a relevant critique of the paper;
- Produce an oral presentation about the two points above.

## Outcome
The work produced was very well received by the teaching team of the course. The oral presentation turned out technical enough to not be boring for experts in the field, while focusing on a high-level overview of the paper. The missing details were exposed in the report.
The teaching team awarded my work a perfect grade.
